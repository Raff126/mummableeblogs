# MUMMABEE BLOG
## Giveaway Page Proposal

Suggested website layout for a reusable MummaBee giveaway page

## What needs to build

The page should work as a reusable campaign template. For each giveaway, the team should only need to change the visual, prize copy, closing date, terms and confirmation message.

### 1. Hero and giveaway image

Campaign name, clear headline, short intro and a large image area for the giveaway visual.

### 2. Prize details

Prize description, eligibility, closing date and how the winner will be contacted.

### 3. Entry form

Full name, social media handle, email address and mobile number. Mobile can be optional if it is not needed.

### 4. Consent and terms

Required checkbox linking to giveaway terms and the privacy notice. Marketing opt in should be separate and optional.

### 5. Submission state

Thank you message after a successful entry, with a link to a related blog post or category.

### 6. Admin side

Securely store entries, include spam protection, and make entries simple for the team to review or export.

## How we use it for the launch

- Launch Reel, post and Story point people to the giveaway page to enter.
- Use reminder Stories during the entry period and on the final day.
- Keep the page as the main entry point, rather than collecting personal details in comments or DMs.
- After entry, link visitors to a useful MummaBee guide so the giveaway also introduces the wider blog.
